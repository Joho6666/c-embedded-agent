# STM32F103C8T6

- Core: ARM Cortex-M3
- Max clock: 72 MHz
- Flash: 64 KB at 0x08000000
- SRAM: 20 KB at 0x20000000
- Package: LQFP48 (C8)
- Debug: SWD (PA13/PA14)

Peripherals: USART1/2/3, ADC1/2, TIM1/2/3/4, I2C1/2, SPI1/2, CAN, USB FS.
